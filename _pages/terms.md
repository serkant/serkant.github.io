This is a personal academic website. It has no comments, forms, accounts, or advertising, and I do not sell or share any information about visitors.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in [GitHub's documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Google Analytics

I use Google Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they read. It sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_*`) and records information such as the pages you view, the referring site, your browser and device type, and your approximate location. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. I only look at aggregate statistics. See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser or by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Third-party scripts

Pages load the MathJax library from [cdnjs](https://cdnjs.com/), a content delivery network run by Cloudflare, which receives standard request information such as your IP address.
